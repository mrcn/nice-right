04 / wording guardrails

Say this. Not that.

Facts first. Ownership and causality require a separate check.

Evidence-ready

"Google Search recorded 1,922 clicks and 68,908 impressions."

It names the system, the property, the dates, and the measured outcome.

Not approved

"Nice Right drove 279% growth."

No intervention date, control series, CRM join, or clean attribution supports that sentence.

Evidence-ready

"574 sessions were tagged Sailthru / email."

This reports what GA4 says without inventing campaign ownership.

Not approved

"The client's newsletter generated 574 sessions."

The campaign operator is unknown, and the row has weak engagement signals.
