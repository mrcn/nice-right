# Nice Right Research Decision Register

Status values describe the state of evidence, not how strongly we feel about a choice.

- **OPEN** — decision is consequential and not established.
- **CANDIDATE** — current material suggests a direction; not validated.
- **BLOCKED** — cannot responsibly decide until named evidence exists.
- **READY FOR TEST** — hypothesis is specific enough to test.
- **APPROVED** — only after the stated gate passes.

## Required research order

D1–D3 must be sufficiently understood before D4–D8 are treated as design inputs.
D4–D6 must be credible before visual direction is allowed to imply promises.
D7–D8 shape the experience architecture. D9 follows the experience hypotheses.
D10 is required before live conversion claims are made.

## Current decision discipline

The existing site and internal documents may supply context, language, assets, and
candidate hypotheses. They may not close D1–D10 by themselves.
