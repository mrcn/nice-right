08 / CLAIM GATEBEFORE PUBLISHING

Every number needs
a claim card.

This is the minimum evidence package before a result moves from research into website copy.

1. Canonical sourceProperty ID, site property, stream, domain, and whether duplicate properties were excluded.required
2. Exact metricSessions, users, clicks, impressions, event count, or downstream contacts: not "traffic" by implication.required
3. Date windowStart/end dates, baseline period, comparison period, and whether the latest window is partial.required
4. MechanismWhat Nice Right changed, when it changed, and why the metric plausibly moved.prove
5. Business outcomeForm, call, booking, sale, or client-confirmed result. GA events alone are tracking signals.prove
6. PermissionClient approval, allowed name/logo, permitted number, and whether the result can be public.prove
Evidence is the product of the research phaseUnverified numbers stay out of the brag bar
